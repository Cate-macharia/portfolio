My story

My first job was in a small shop in Molo, Kenya. I was the cashier. I was also the one they called when the computer broke.

A few years later I was studying computer science and writing code for a living, and I kept thinking about shops like that one. Most of them still ran on a notebook and somebody's memory.

So at 22, with two co-founders, I started Flexbyte Solutions. We build simple systems for Kenyan businesses: tills that take M-Pesa, rent records for landlords, trip and fuel tracking for lorry owners. One of our clients used to write every invoice by hand. Now he tells people he can track every shilling.

Today I also work as a software engineer at AfCEN, building the Deal Room, where African businesses meet investors.

I am still at the beginning. If you would like to hear the rest, save my contact and let's talk.

What I've built

Products I have designed and developed, and platforms I have worked on.

  • Logistics management system

    Flexbyte Solutions, for Stepstar Logistics

    Live with a client

    A haulage management platform covering orders and shipments, zone-based rate pricing, VAT-compliant invoicing, driver and vehicle management, and live GPS vehicle tracking with route history. It is built so that many transport companies can run on the same platform, each with its own private data.

  • Flexbyte POS

    Flexbyte Solutions

    Demo available

    A point-of-sale and retail management platform for hardware shops, chemists, agrovets, bars and restaurants, and general retail. It has separate dashboards for five user roles, stock control with barcode lookup, supplier and credit-customer accounts, payroll, and a fuel station module with shift reconciliation. A cashier can send an M-Pesa payment request from the till, and the sale is reconciled automatically when the customer pays.
