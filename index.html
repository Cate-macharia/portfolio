About

I'm a software developer and co-founder of FlexByte Solutions, where I design and build full-stack systems for small and medium businesses across Kenya — from point-of-sale software for retail shops and fuel stations, to property management platforms for landlords and caretakers, to logistics software for haulage companies. I work across the stack: Laravel APIs, React/TypeScript frontends, MySQL schema design, and integrations like the Safaricom M-Pesa Daraja API that these businesses actually depend on.

Projects

FlexByte POS

Backend Frontend

A multi-tenant point-of-sale and retail management platform for Kenyan SMEs — hardware shops, chemists, agrovets, bars & restaurants, fuel stations, and general retail. Role-based dashboards for Super Admin, Manager, Cashier, Storekeeper, and Salesperson; inventory with barcode lookup and configurable selling units; supplier and credit-customer management; payroll; and a full fuel station module with tank/shift reconciliation.

Live M-Pesa Daraja integration — STK Push from the till, status polling, and automatic payment reconciliation against the sale.
